Privacy Policy

AllAbout Bible

1. Our approach

AllAbout Bible ("the app") does not require an account and does not collect your personal information on any server run by the developer. When you use certain features, some information is stored or sent to outside services as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Notes, sermons, highlights, handwriting, bookmarks, worship setlists, settings and other data you create are stored on your device.

To activate paid content, the app creates an identifier from device information and keeps it on your device only. It is never sent automatically. The developer receives it only if you send the "request code" shown in the app when you ask for activation.

3. Cloud sync (optional)

If you turn on sync, your data is saved to your own iCloud, Google Drive or Dropbox account so it can sync across devices and be restored when you reinstall the app. This data is stored in your account and the developer cannot see it. The privacy policy of each service (Apple, Google or Dropbox) applies to data stored there.

If sync is on, deleting the app does not delete the data in your cloud account. To remove it completely, please also delete it from that cloud service.

4. Information sent when you use outside services

When you use the following features, the information needed for that feature is sent to the service that provides it.

  • Finding sheet music, lyrics or YouTube videos: your search terms, such as a song title, are sent to Naver, Google, YouTube or Bugs.
  • YouTube playback: the app connects to YouTube to play videos.
  • Maps: the app connects to map services such as OpenTopoMap to load map images.
  • Document import: the app downloads files from an address you enter, such as GitHub.

The app does not send your name, contact details or other information that identifies you. As with any internet connection, these services may record connection information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising and analytics

The app does not use advertising SDKs or separate analytics tools. Basic app statistics provided by Apple and Google may be processed under their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing with third parties

Except where required by law, the app does not share or sell your information to third parties, and does not outsource the processing of personal information.

7. Your rights

  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device.
  • Data synced to the cloud can be deleted from that cloud service.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when laws or the app change. We will announce changes in the app or on this page.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us.
